These are candidate EU-cyber/OSS outlets for the ImpactPrism story. They are intentionally listed without URLs or contact details: verify that each is active, relevant, and accepting pitches immediately before each send. No outreach has been sent.

| Candidate | Angle | Required pre-send verification |

| EU Cyber Direct newsletter | EU cyber-policy and practical resilience angle | Confirm current publication activity, editorial scope, and accepted submission route |

## Verification checklist before each send

1. Visit the outlet’s current official publication page through an independently verified source.
2. Confirm a recent issue or post date and that the outlet is still active.
3. Confirm that the subject fits the outlet’s cyber, OSS, or EU software-supply-chain focus.
4. Confirm the current editor, form, or submission instructions; do not guess an email address.
5. Replace the local demo link in [newsletter-pitch.md](newsletter-pitch.md) with a verified public link.
6. Record the verification date and source in the working outreach log before sending.

Primary CTA for any published version: install from GitHub, then run
`impactprism scan .`.

Feedback loop: [open an issue](https://github.com/bulltickr/impactprism/issues) with what you scanned, what you expected, and what is missing.
