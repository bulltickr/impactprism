# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participants must be
respectful, assume good faith, describe disagreements with evidence, and keep
feedback focused on behavior and its impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.
Reports should be sent privately through the contact route described in
`SECURITY.md`.
